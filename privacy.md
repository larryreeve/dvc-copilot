# DVC Copilot Privacy Policy

Effective November 25, 2026

DVC Copilot is an independently developed planning app. You can use it without creating an account. It includes no advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs.

## Information stored on your device

Your saved stays, notes, search details, and any previously entered contract or point-balance information are stored on your device. These details are not automatically sent to the developer.

Your device’s backup settings may include this information. Deleting the app removes its local data. Copies in device backups or files you exported may remain until you delete them separately.

## Optional feedback

When you send feedback, the app sends your message, feedback category, app version, iOS version, and an app-identification label to the developer through Formspree. This information helps investigate problems and improve the app.

The feedback form does not ask for your name or email address. Saved stays, notes, search details, and diagnostic traces are not automatically attached. Please avoid including sensitive information in your message.

Formspree processes submissions under its [privacy policy](https://formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy/).

## Connections to other services

The app downloads public point-chart information from Disney and checks a public file on GitHub for app-update requirements. These requests do not include your saved stays, notes, search details, or contract information.

These services receive ordinary connection information, such as your IP address and request headers. Their handling of that information is governed by their own privacy policies.

## Local diagnostics

The app stores technical details about the latest point-chart update on your device. These can include request URLs and headers, response codes and headers, response sizes, text responses, and errors.

These records help explain update failures. They are replaced by the next update attempt and are not automatically sent to the developer. Binary PDF response bodies are not stored in the diagnostic trace.

## Sharing

When you share a stay, the information you choose to share—including saved notes—is provided to your selected destination. Exported planning backups also contain app data. The receiving app or service handles that information under its own privacy practices.

## Optional voice and Copilot features

On supported devices, optional Copilot features use Apple’s on-device language model to interpret planning requests. The app’s voice-input feature uses on-device speech recognition and requires permission to access the microphone and speech recognition.

The app does not send your voice recordings or planning messages to the developer. You can change these permissions in iOS Settings.

## Retention and deletion of feedback

Feedback is retained to investigate issues and improve the app, and deleted when it is no longer needed.

You can request deletion through **About DVC Copilot → Send feedback**. Include the approximate submission date and enough of the original message to help locate it. Because the form does not require an account or contact address, some submissions may be difficult to identify.

## Privacy questions

For privacy questions or deletion requests, use **About DVC Copilot → Send feedback**.

You can also use the [DVC Copilot support page](https://github.com/larryreeve/dvc-copilot/blob/main/support.md). GitHub issues are public; do not post personal or sensitive information there.

## Policy changes

This policy may be updated when the app’s features or data practices change. The effective date above identifies the latest revision.
