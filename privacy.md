# DVC Copilot Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026

DVC Copilot does not require an account and includes no advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs.

## Stored on your device

Saved stays, notes, search details, and previously entered contract and point-balance information are stored locally. Device backups may include this data. Removing the app removes its local data; restoring a backup may restore it. The app does not automatically send these details to the developer.

## Optional feedback

If you choose Send feedback, your message, feedback type, app version, iOS version, and the source label “DVC Copilot iOS app” are sent to the developer through Formspree. Saved stays, notes, and search details are not automatically attached. Avoid including personal or sensitive information in your message. Formspree processes submissions under its [privacy policy](https://formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy/). Feedback is used to investigate problems and improve the app.

## Network connections

Point-chart updates retrieve public information from Disney. The app checks a public policy file hosted on GitHub to determine whether an app update is required. These requests do not include saved stays or contract information. As with other internet requests, service providers receive network information such as your IP address. Opening external links is subject to those websites’ privacy policies.

## Sharing and optional planning tools

When you share a stay or export a backup, the destination you select receives that information. On supported devices, optional chat interpretation uses Apple’s on-device language model. No chat text is automatically sent to the developer.

## Contact

For support or privacy questions, use Send feedback in About DVC Copilot or open an issue at:

https://github.com/larryreeve/dvc-copilot/issues

GitHub issues are public. Do not include personal information.

DVC Copilot is an unofficial planning app and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Disney or Disney Vacation Club.
