# DVC Copilot Planning Information

## Independent planning tool

DVC Copilot is a free, independently developed planning tool. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by The Walt Disney Company or Disney Vacation Club.

## Estimates, not reservations

Point costs are estimates based on published charts and the dates and rooms you select. Information may contain errors or become outdated. The app does not check live availability, make reservations, or move points in your Disney account.

## Confirm before acting

Verify point requirements, contract restrictions, balances, and deadlines directly with Disney Vacation Club before booking, banking, borrowing, or making travel purchases.

## Accuracy and responsibility

DVC Copilot and its data, calculations, estimates, and suggestions are provided “as is” and “as available” for informational and planning purposes only. Accuracy, completeness, reliability, and timeliness are not guaranteed. You are responsible for independently verifying information and for your booking, point-use, purchasing, and travel decisions. Do not rely on the app as your sole source of information.

## Planning scope

Search results compare point costs. They do not confirm whether your contracts or points qualify for a booking. Contract management and reminders are not part of this version.
